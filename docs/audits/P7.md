## Outcome

`qwen2.5:7b-instruct` reliably produces _valid, on-style_ SVG: strokes only, round caps, inside the grid, after at most two corrections. But the drawings are abstract glyphs, not recognisable pictures of the concept (see the contact sheets): no bee, turtle, harp or sheep is identifiable. The vision judge agrees: 3 of 141 "fit", all for Origami, where abstract folded shapes pass. On my own review I would approve none, so `generated/approved.json` stays empty and no generated set ships. Per §15.7 a low rate is a valid outcome.

The validation, gallery, judge and approval path is in place, so a stronger drawing model can be dropped in via `--model`. Candidates to try, at the owner's discretion: a larger local model (e.g. a 32B coder model) or a hosted model, which would cost money and is a hard checkpoint.
